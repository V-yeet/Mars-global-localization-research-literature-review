# Mars-global-localization-research-literature-review
Research project: The Architecture of Mars Global Localization for the Perseverance Rover: Onboard Map Matching and Hardware Repurposing



Academic Research Paper : The Architecture of Mars Global Localization for the Perseverance Rover

This paper was developed as part of a university coursework project at THWS to gain experience in scientific research and academic writing. It examines the architecture of Mars Global Localization for NASA's Perseverance rover, focusing on onboard image matching, autonomous localization, and hardware repurposing.

Scope

The paper synthesizes and analyzes information from existing scientific publications and technical sources to explore:
The challenges of odometry drift and communication delays in Mars rover navigation.
The use of onboard camera imagery and orbital maps for global localization.
Hardware adaptation to support computationally intensive localization tasks.
The software architecture and reported performance of Mars Global Localization.

Learning Objectives

The project provided experience in reviewing technical literature, organizing complex engineering concepts, synthesizing information from multiple sources, and structuring a scientific paper.

Authors

Vihaan Yogish, Ahillan Mohankumar, Vineet Mukund Khare, Nischal Shivanand Indi, and Mokshitha Karnati.
Institution: Technische Hochschule Würzburg-Schweinfurt (THWS)
